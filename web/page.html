How it works

Each layer is independent, so new sources and new checks plug in without touching the rest.

Connectors

Shopify, Google Ads, Meta, GA4 today. Merchant Center and TikTok are one file each.

Canonical model

Orders, refunds, products with landed cost, ad spend per campaign and day, traffic.

src/core/types.ts
03

Profit engine

CM1 → CM2 → CM3 per product, campaign and channel. Ad spend allocated by Shopify-confirmed revenue.

src/engine/profit.ts

Detectors

Loss-makers, capped winners, CPC spikes, stock-outs, attribution gaps, shipping leaks. Hourly, with Slack alerts.

src/engine/insights.ts

AI analyst
